Course Overview

Electricity is the backbone of modern infrastructure. This course is heavily focused on practical, hands-on learning rather than just theory. You will spend 80% of your time in the workshop.

Students are trained on strict safety protocols, learning to assemble, install, test, and maintain electrical or electronic wiring, equipment, and fixtures using modern tools.

What You Will Learn

Domestic Wiring

House wiring circuits, switchboards, MCB installations, and understanding load distribution.

Industrial Applications

3-phase motors, industrial panel wiring, contactors, and heavy machinery circuits.

Appliance Repair

Diagnosis and repair of common household appliances like geysers, fans, and motors.

Safety & Testing

Earthing techniques, using multimeters, insulation testing, and strict safety guidelines.
