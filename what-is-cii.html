Confederation of Indian Industry (CII)

Driving India's industrial excellence and fostering a future-ready, industry-aligned skill development ecosystem since 1895.

CII & CII CoE Skills

The **Confederation of Indian Industry (CII)** is a non-governmental, not-for-profit, industry-led, and industry-managed organization. Acting as a major catalyst for India's economic growth, it plays an influential role in policy advocacy, trade partnerships, and human capital development.

Through the **CII - Rahul Bajaj Centre of Excellence on Skills** (CII CoE Skills), CII serves as a critical bridge between industry opportunities and youth aspirations. Active in the national skills agenda since 1987, CII implements diverse, scalable models to upgrade training quality and ensure sustainable employment for millions of Indian youth.

Established 1895 10M Youth Target Industry-Led CSR Collaborator

Centres of Excellence Model

Established starting in 1995 to drive industry competitiveness, capability building, and specialized skill delivery.

Model Career Centres (MCCs)

Operating since 2016 in partnership with the government to provide job-aligned counseling and placement assistance.

National Standardization

Aligning vocational curricula with the National Skills Qualifications Framework (NSQF) for validated credits.

Core Operational Objectives

Bridging the industry-talent mismatch through systematic, national-level interventions.

Bridging the Skill Gap

Designing structured skilling, re-skilling, and up-skilling programs to equip youth with trades currently in demand across key economic sectors.

Talent Sourcing

Leveraging specialized "Recruit-Train-Deploy" (RTD) models to map employer requirements directly to candidate mobilization pipelines.

Employment Placement

Working alongside Model Career Centres to provide counseling, soft skills, and direct interviews, enabling seamless entry into the workforce.

Standardized Curricula

Structuring training to match NSQF standards and facilitating government-validated, credit-based certifications for students.

Policy Partnering

Serving as a strategic partner to MSDE, MoE, and MoLE on national skill policies, including the Prime Minister's Internship Scheme.

Inclusive Human Capital

Expanding vocational reach to marginalized groups, rural youth, and women, prioritizing development in India's aspirational districts.

Multi-Skill Training Institutes (MSTI)

A flagship model driving multi-corporate, multi-trade vocational excellence.

The MSTI Delivery Model

CII Multi-Skill Training Institutes operate on a unique **multi-corporate, multi-skilling model** designed to address the specific job needs of local youth while catering to different industry sectors under one roof.

These institutes combine high-end vocational training, government sponsorships, and corporate CSR funding to run specialized programs in manufacturing, hospitality, IT-ITeS, retail, logistics, and technical trades.

Industry Alignment

Curriculum is reviewed continuously by partnering corporates to ensure relevant, up-to-date training.

State-of-the-Art Labs

Equipped with advanced technical setups and machinery for hands-on, practical expertise.

Placement Support

Direct placement linkages and employer tie-ups to ensure immediate hiring post-completion.

CSR Collaborations

Supported by leading corporate social responsibility initiatives and municipal agreements.
