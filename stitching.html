Course Overview

For over 170 years, Singer India has been synonymous with sewing excellence. We have partnered with this legendary brand to bring you a world-class Stitching & Tailoring program right here in Dharamshala.

Whether you want to stitch clothes for your family, work in the fashion industry, or become an independent entrepreneur by opening your own boutique, this hands-on course provides you with the technical expertise and confidence you need. You will learn on modern, high-quality Singer machines under the guidance of expert instructors.

What You Will Learn

Pattern Making & Cutting

Learn precise body measurements, drafting patterns, and cutting fabrics accurately for various body types.

Garment Construction

Master the stitching of traditional Indian wear (suits, kurtis, blouses) and modern western garments.

Machine Operation

Complete training on operating, maintaining, and troubleshooting modern Singer sewing machines.

Embroidery & Finishing

Add value to garments with intricate hand embroidery, machine embroidery, and professional finishing techniques.

Start Your Fashion Journey

Transform your creativity into a profession. Admissions are open for the current batch. Limited seats available to ensure personalized machine time.
