Course Overview

The digital revolution has transformed every industry, making IT skills indispensable for career growth. Our IT & Computing course, in strategic partnership with the NIIT Foundation, offers a rigorous curriculum designed for students who want to excel in modern corporate environments.

Through hands-on practical training in our state-of-the-art computer labs, students will learn to operate operating systems, master office productivity suites, understand fundamental coding logic, and manage network security basics.

What You Will Learn

Computer Fundamentals

Hardware, Software, OS basics, and understanding how computers process data.

Office Productivity Suites

Advanced MS Word formatting, Excel logic/formulas, and PowerPoint presentations.

Internet & Networking

Navigating the web safely, email etiquette, and basic network troubleshooting.

Coding Logic & Basics

Introduction to programming concepts, variables, and logic gates.
