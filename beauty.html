Course Overview

The beauty and wellness sector is one of the fastest-growing industries globally. To meet the high demand for trained professionals, our Beautician program is strictly aligned with the guidelines of the Beauty & Wellness Sector Skill Council (BWSSC).

Students will receive comprehensive hands-on training covering everything from basic grooming to advanced bridal makeup artistry. You will practice in our fully-equipped modern salon labs, ensuring you build the confidence needed to serve high-end clients or manage your own successful salon.

What You Will Learn

Professional Makeup

Master everyday looks, party makeup, airbrush techniques, and complete bridal makeovers.

Hair Styling & Care

Learn advanced hair cutting, coloring, chemical treatments, and creative styling for events.

Skincare & Facials

Understand skin types and perform professional facials, cleanups, waxing, and threading safely.

Salon Management

Client communication, hygiene standards, inventory management, and business operations.
