Course Overview

The construction and infrastructure sector is booming, leading to a massive shortage of certified heavy machinery operators. Our JCB Trainer program is a fast-tracked, intensive course.

To ensure absolute safety and mastery, students first practice on state-of-the-art VR simulators before moving to our secure outdoor site for live hands-on operation of actual JCB excavators.

What You Will Learn

Introduction of JCB

Representing the pinnacle of engineering excellence, JCB machines seamlessly blend innovation with unparalleled efficiency.

Maintenance of Machine

Regular maintenance is the silent conductor ensuring the symphony of machinery plays on, harmonizing efficiency and longevity.

Basic Hydraulics

Flowing through the veins of innovation, Basic Hydraulics in JCB machines orchestrates the graceful dance of power and precision.

Health, Safety & First Aid

Health, Safety, and First Aid are the sturdy pillars upon which every JCB endeavor stands. With diligence and care, we safeguard not just equipment.

Loader and Operation

Like a symphony conductor directing every note, the JCB loader gracefully orchestrates the dance of earth and machine.

Excavator and Operation

Amidst the chaos, the excavator emerges as the guardian of safety, orchestrating a symphony of precision in the delicate dance of operation.
