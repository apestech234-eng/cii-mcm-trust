Course Overview

The construction and infrastructure sector is booming, leading to a massive shortage of certified heavy machinery operators. Our JCB Trainer program is a fast-tracked, intensive course.

To ensure absolute safety and mastery, students first practice on state-of-the-art VR simulators before moving to our secure outdoor site for live hands-on operation of actual JCB excavators.

What You Will Learn

VR Simulation

Master controls and spatial awareness safely on digital simulators before operating real machines.

Trenching & Loading

Real-world practice of digging, trenching, material loading, and site leveling.

Daily Maintenance

Learn to perform vital daily checks, greasing, and minor mechanical troubleshooting.

Safety Regulations
